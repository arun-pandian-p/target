## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Don't wait for perfect data** — synthetic data is fine. Employers care about your thinking, not the data source.
2. **Title your repos with company names** — recruiters Google "[Company] + data analyst" and your repo shows up.
3. **Comment your code** — analysts who explain their thinking get hired. Coders who don't, don't.
4. **Each report should have at least 1 chart** — visuals make reports shareable on LinkedIn.
5. **Tag the company's official LinkedIn page** in your post — their employees will see it.
6. **Cross-post to:** LinkedIn + GitHub README + your portfolio blog (arunpandian.online)
