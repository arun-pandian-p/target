# 🤝 STRATEGY 4 — NETWORK & INDUSTRY FRIEND APPROACH
## Arun Pandian | Connect Like a Pro, Not Like a Job Seeker

> **Mindset Shift:** Don't approach people asking for a job.
> Approach them as a peer who does interesting work. Jobs follow naturally.

---

## 🧠 THE CORE PRINCIPLE: GIVE FIRST, GET LATER

Most people DM: *"Hi, I'm looking for a job. Can you refer me?"*
→ 95% get ignored.

You will DM: *"Hi, I saw your post about [X]. I built something similar. Here's what I found..."*
→ 60% get a response. Why? Because you brought value first.

## 🎯 THE REFERRAL UNLOCK FORMULA

To get a referral from someone you barely know:

1. **Engage with them** for 2 weeks (comment on 3–4 of their posts)
2. **Share their post** with your audience and tag them
3. **DM them:** "Hey [Name], I shared your recent post on [topic] — really resonated with my work on [X]"
4. **Build connection** for 1 more week
5. **DM:** "I see [Company] is hiring for Data Analyst. Since you're there, would you be open to referring me? I've built [specific relevant project]."

At this point, they KNOW you, they've seen your work, and the referral feels natural — not a cold ask.
